# Overview — the way in

**Overview**, the first entry of the Nomajde menu, is where the application opens: **the way in**. One group of links per thing a person comes to do — look at the platform, prepare an upgrade, consult the JD Edwards data, maintain the security, explore — and the jobs with their last run. The figures of the platform are in [Platform synthesis](./synthesis.md), those of the upgrade in [Prepare for upgrade](./upgrade/analysis.md).
